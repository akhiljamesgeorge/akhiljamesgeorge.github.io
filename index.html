About

Technology matters most when it becomes useful.

I am an engineer with a strong interest in innovation, product development and the technologies reshaping how industrial products and services are built.

My experience spans R&D projects, cross-functional collaboration and emerging technologies including digitalization, servitization, IoT, AI and data science. I enjoy connecting technical possibilities with real business needs and moving ideas from exploration toward implementation.

Alongside the technology itself, I care deeply about the people behind it: building capable teams, creating clarity in complex projects and fostering a culture of curiosity and continuous improvement.
